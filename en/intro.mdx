## Why It Matters

Children become interested in video games and electronic devices at an early age. They learn to use them quickly.

Parents know that information technologies matter for their child's development. They also want to keep a balance between learning and health.

![kids](../images/intro/kids.png)

Research shows that early and frequent interaction with screens can reduce cognitive abilities and academic performance.

![graph](../images/intro/graph.jpg)

[_Source: Programme for International Student Assessment (PISA), 2022 Results (Volume I)_](https://www.oecd-ilibrary.org/education/pisa-2022-results-volume-i_53f23881-en)

When young children use screens a lot, it often leads to these problems:

- psychological difficulties,
- gaming addiction,
- deterioration of vision and physical health.

## Goals and Objectives

With PrimaSTEM, children from age 4 learn programming, logic, and mathematics **without screens**.

What children master with PrimaSTEM:

- numbers,
- spatial orientation,
- algorithms,
- logical thinking,
- programming fundamentals,
- arithmetic operations and progressions,
- geometric concepts.

Why PrimaSTEM is convenient:

- it suits different subjects and tasks,
- lessons are engaging and visual,
- learning happens without screens,
- it suits preschool and early school-age children,
- it is made of natural materials.

> 🎯 **Main goal.** The child develops cognitive skills. The child touches and sees how a program is built and understands what the result of running it means.

## How It Works

1. Turn on the robot and the control board.
2. Place the command tokens in the cells of the control board. This creates a movement program.
3. Press the **▶ Start** button, and the robot runs the program.

## Who It Is For

PrimaSTEM is designed for children and looks like a game. Educators and parents can use it as a flexible tool. It works for teaching mathematics, programming, physics, history, and geography. Only the imagination and skill of the teacher or parents set the limits.

The child gets a foundation in mathematics and algorithms. This is good preparation for school and for the first programming languages: Scratch, Logo, or Minecraft.
