## Relevance

**Modern children** from an early age show interest in video games and electronic devices, quickly mastering them.

Parents understand the importance of information technologies for the successful development of their child, but at the same time strive to maintain a balance between learning and children's health.

![kids](../images/kids.png)

Research shows that early and frequent interaction with screens can reduce cognitive abilities and academic performance.

![graph](../images/graph.jpg)

*Source: Programme for International Student Assessment (PISA), [2022 Results (Volume I)](https://www.oecd-ilibrary.org/education/pisa-2022-results-volume-i_53f23881-en)*

The use of screen devices by young children often leads to:
- psychological difficulties,
- gaming addiction,
- deterioration of vision and physical health.

## Goals and Objectives

This gaming **tool** helps children from age 4 learn programming, logic, and mathematics **without screens**.

With PrimaSTEM, the following are mastered:
- numbers,
- spatial orientation,
- algorithms,
- logical thinking,
- programming basics,
- arithmetic operations and progressions,
- geometric concepts.

**Advantages:**
- versatility of application,
- interesting and visual format,
- screen-free learning,
- suitable for preschool and elementary school age children,
- natural body materials.

> 🎯 **Main goal** — development of cognitive skills through tangible and visual understanding of programming basics and the meaning of program execution results.

## How Does It Work?

1. Turn on the robot and the control panel.
2. Using command tokens, create a program by placing them in the panel cells.
3. Press the "Execute" button.
4. The robot will execute the program.

## Who Is It For?

PrimaSTEM is designed for children and looks like a game, but it's a flexible tool for educators and parents. It can be used to teach various subjects — mathematics, programming, physics, history, geography. Everything is limited only by the imagination and skill of the teacher or parents.

The child acquires a mathematical and algorithmic foundation, which becomes excellent preparation for school and first experience with programming languages (Scratch, Logo, or Minecraft).
