## 3. ⚠ Safety warnings

**WARNING.** Not suitable for children under 36 months. Small parts — choking hazard.

**WARNING.** Charge only under adult supervision, with a CE-certified USB charger (5 V).

**WARNING.** Do not disassemble, crush, puncture, short-circuit, burn or open the device. The battery is not user-replaceable.

**WARNING.** If the device heats abnormally, deforms, leaks or emits an odour while charging, unplug immediately. Do not charge again.

**WARNING.** Do not expose to water or humidity.

## 4. Battery information

Two integrated lithium-ion batteries (one in the robot, one in the remote). Chemistry: Li-ion. Size: 16340. Nominal voltage: 3.6 V. Not user-replaceable.

|---|---|
| 2 400 – 2 483.5 MHz (Bluetooth Low Energy) | −1 dBm (0.79 mW) EIRP |
| 13.56 MHz (NFC, inductive) | very low power (below evaluation threshold) |

The device may be used in all EU Member States.

## 6. Disposal

The crossed-out wheeled bin symbol *with bar* means the device must not be disposed of with household waste — hand it to an authorised electronic waste collection point.

The crossed-out wheeled bin symbol *without bar* means batteries must be collected separately. Do not remove the batteries yourself — hand the complete unit over.
